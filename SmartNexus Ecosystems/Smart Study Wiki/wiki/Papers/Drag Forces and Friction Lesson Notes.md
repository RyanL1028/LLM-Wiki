## Summary

This source focuses on [[Friction]] and [[Drag Force]], including how each affects motion, how to reduce or increase them, and where resistive forces are useful or harmful.

## Core Ideas

- Friction acts between surfaces and opposes motion.
- Drag acts through air/water and increases resistance.
- Streamlining and lubrication reduce resistive forces.
- Resistive forces influence resultant force and therefore speed changes.
