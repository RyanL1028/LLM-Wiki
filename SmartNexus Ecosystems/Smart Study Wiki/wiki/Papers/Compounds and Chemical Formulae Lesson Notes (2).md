## Summary

This source reinforces the differences between mixtures and compounds, reviews chemical symbols, and focuses on writing and interpreting chemical formulae. It explains fixed composition of compounds and how formulae show relative atom numbers.

## Core Ideas

- Mixtures: substances not chemically joined, easy to separate, keep individual properties.
- Compounds: atoms chemically joined, fixed composition, different properties from elements.
- Chemical symbols represent elements with 1-2 letters.
- Formulae show relative numbers of atoms; subscripts indicate multiples.

## Teaching and Assessment Focus

- Distinguish mixtures from compounds using properties and separation methods.
- Recall and apply chemical symbols.
- Write and interpret chemical formulae for common compounds.
- Identify elements and atom counts in formulae.
