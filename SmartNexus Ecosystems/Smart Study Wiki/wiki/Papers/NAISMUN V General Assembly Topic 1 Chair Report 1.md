## Summary

This chair report addresses access to healthcare in conflict regions, highlighting challenges such as infrastructure destruction, security risks, and resource shortages. It discusses international efforts, legal frameworks, and proposed solutions for improving medical aid delivery in war zones.

## Core Ideas

- Conflict regions face severe healthcare access barriers due to violence, displacement, and damaged facilities.
- International humanitarian law and organizations like WHO, Red Cross play key roles.
- Solutions include protected medical zones, telemedicine, and coordinated aid efforts.
- Case studies from recent conflicts illustrate successes and failures.

## Teaching and Assessment Focus

- Analyze the impact of conflict on healthcare systems.
- Evaluate international responses and legal obligations.
- Propose policy recommendations for better access.
