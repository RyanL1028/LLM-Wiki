## Summary

This source defines combustion as rapid oxidation releasing heat and light, explains fuels and their types, and teaches writing word equations for combustion reactions. It evaluates fossil fuels vs renewable alternatives and their environmental impacts.

## Core Ideas

- Combustion is a rapid chemical reaction with oxygen producing heat, light, and often flames.
- Fuels are substances that burn to release energy; classified as fossil (non-renewable) or renewable.
- Word equations show reactants (fuels + oxygen) and products (CO2, H2O, etc.).
- Fossil fuels contribute to pollution and climate change; renewables like biofuels are sustainable.

## Teaching and Assessment Focus

- Define combustion and distinguish from slower oxidation.
- Identify fuels and their sources.
- Write word equations for combustion reactions.
- Evaluate advantages and disadvantages of different fuels.
