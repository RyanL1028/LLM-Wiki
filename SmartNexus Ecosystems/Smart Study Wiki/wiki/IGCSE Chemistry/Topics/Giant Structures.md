## Comparison of Giant Structures

| Property | Giant Ionic | Giant Covalent (Diamond) | Giant Covalent (Graphite) | Giant Metallic |
|----------|------------|-------------------------|--------------------------|----------------|
| Particles | Positive + negative ions | Atoms | Atoms | Positive ions + delocalised e⁻ |
| Bonding | Electrostatic attraction | Covalent bonds | Covalent bonds + weak inter-layer forces | Electrostatic attraction to e⁻ sea |
| Melting point | High | Very high | Very high | High |
| Conducts electricity? | Only when molten/aqueous | No | Yes | Yes (solid + liquid) |
| Hardness | Hard but brittle | Very hard | Soft (layers slide) | Malleable/ductile |
| Soluble in water? | Many are | No | No | No |

## How to Answer Comparison Questions

**Q: Explain why X has a higher melting point than Y.**

1. State the type of structure for each substance
2. State what particles are present
3. State which forces/bonds are overcome during melting
4. Compare the relative strengths
5. Conclude

**Example**: "NaCl has a higher melting point than H₂O because NaCl has a giant ionic lattice with strong electrostatic forces between ions that require a lot of energy to overcome, whereas H₂O has a simple molecular structure with only weak intermolecular forces between molecules."

## Key Facts
- Giant structures have high melting points because strong bonds hold the structure together throughout
- Simple molecular substances have low melting points because only weak intermolecular forces need to be overcome
- The type of bonding determines ALL physical properties
