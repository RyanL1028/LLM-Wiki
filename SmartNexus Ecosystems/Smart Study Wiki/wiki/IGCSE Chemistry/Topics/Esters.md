# Esters

**Summary**: Formed from carboxylic acid + alcohol (esterification). Have fruity smells. Used as flavourings, fragrances, and solvents.

## What Are Esters?

Esters have the functional group **—COO—** and general formula RCOOR'.

## Formation (Esterification)

Carboxylic acid + Alcohol ⇌ Ester + Water

This is a **condensation** reaction (water eliminated) and is **reversible**.

## Properties
- **Fruity/sweet smells** — characteristic of each ester
- **Volatile** — evaporate easily
- **Colourless liquids** at room temperature
- **Insoluble in water**

## Naming Esters

Ester name = [alcohol part]-yl [acid part]-oate

| Alcohol | Acid | Ester | Smell |
|---------|------|-------|-------|
| Ethanol | Ethanoic acid | Ethyl ethanoate | Pear drops |
| Methanol | Butanoic acid | Methyl butanoate | Apple |
| Ethanol | Methanoic acid | Ethyl methanoate | Raspberry |
| Pentanol | Ethanoic acid | Pentyl ethanoate | Banana |

## Uses
- **Flavourings** in food and drinks
- **Fragrances** in perfumes and cosmetics
- **Solvents** for glues, inks, nail varnish remover
- **Plasticisers** to make plastics more flexible

## Key Facts
- Esterification = acid + alcohol → ester + water
- Concentrated H₂SO₄ is the catalyst
- The reaction is REVERSIBLE
- Esters have characteristic fruity smells
