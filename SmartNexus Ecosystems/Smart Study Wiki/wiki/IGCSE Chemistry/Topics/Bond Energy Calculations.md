# Bond Energy Calculations

**Summary**: ΔH = sum of bond energies broken − sum of bond energies formed. Used to calculate the enthalpy change of a reaction.
**Tags**: #igcse #chemistry #energetics
**Created**: 2026-07-14
**Last Updated**: 2026-07-14

---

## Formula

**ΔH = Σ(bond energies of bonds BROKEN) − Σ(bond energies of bonds FORMED)**

## Method

1. Write the balanced equation with displayed (structural) formulae showing ALL bonds
2. List all bonds broken in reactants and sum their bond energies
3. List all bonds formed in products and sum their bond energies
4. Apply the formula

## Worked Example: Combustion of Methane

CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O

**Bonds broken (reactants):**
- 4 × C−H = 4 × 413 = 1652 kJ
- 2 × O=O = 2 × 498 = 996 kJ
- Total broken = 2648 kJ

**Bonds formed (products):**
- 2 × C=O = 2 × 799 = 1598 kJ
- 4 × O−H = 4 × 464 = 1856 kJ
- Total formed = 3454 kJ

**ΔH = 2648 − 3454 = −806 kJ/mol** (exothermic)

## Key Bond Energies to Know

| Bond | Energy (kJ/mol) |
|------|----------------|
| C−H | 413 |
| C−C | 347 |
| C=C | 614 |
| O=O | 498 |
| O−H | 464 |
| C=O | 799 |
| H−H | 436 |
| Cl−Cl | 243 |
| H−Cl | 432 |

## Key Facts
- Bond BREAKING absorbs energy (endothermic)
- Bond MAKING releases energy (exothermic)
- A reaction is exothermic overall if more energy is released making bonds than absorbed breaking them
- These bond energies are **average** values
