# Group 1 Alkali Metals

**Summary**: Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs — soft, highly reactive metals. Reactivity increases down the group. React vigorously with water forming alkaline hydroxides and H₂ gas.

## Properties of Group 1 Metals

| Property | Trend down group |
|----------|-----------------|
| Atomic radius | Increases |
| Density | Generally increases |
| Melting/boiling point | Decreases |
| Reactivity | **Increases** |
| Softness | Increases |

## Why Reactivity Increases Down Group 1

- Atomic radius increases → outer electron is further from nucleus
- Increased shielding by inner electron shells
- Outer electron is **more easily lost** → more reactive
- Francium (Fr) would be the most reactive, but is radioactive and rare

## Reaction with Water

**General equation**: 2M(s) + 2H₂O(l) → 2MOH(aq) + H₂(g)

| Metal | Observations with water |
|-------|------------------------|
| Lithium (Li) | Fizzes gently, floats, moves on surface |
| Sodium (Na) | Fizzes vigorously, melts into a ball, darts around |
| Potassium (K) | Reacts violently, ignites with lilac flame, explodes |

**The solution formed is the metal hydroxide — strongly alkaline** (pH ~13–14). Turns universal indicator purple.

## Reaction with Oxygen
- 4Li + O₂ → 2Li₂O (lithium oxide)
- 4Na + O₂ → 2Na₂O (sodium oxide)
- 4K + O₂ → 2K₂O (potassium oxide)

## Reaction with Halogens
- 2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl (vigorous reaction)
- All produce white crystalline metal halides

## Key Facts
- Group 1 metals are stored under OIL to prevent reaction with air/water
- They are soft enough to cut with a knife
- Reactivity INCREASES down the group (opposite to Group 7)
- All form 1+ ions (lose one outer electron)
- The hydroxide solution is alkaline — hence the name "alkali metals"
