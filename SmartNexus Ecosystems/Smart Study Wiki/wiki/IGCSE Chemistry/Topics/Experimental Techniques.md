## Separation Methods

### Filtration
Separates an **insoluble solid** from a liquid.
- Mixture poured through filter paper in a funnel
- Solid (residue) stays on filter paper; liquid (filtrate) passes through
- Example: sand from water, BaSO₄ precipitate from solution

### Crystallisation / Evaporation
Separates a **dissolved solid** from a solution to obtain pure crystals.
- Gently heat the solution to evaporate some solvent
- Allow to cool → crystals form as solubility decreases
- Filter and dry the crystals
- Example: obtaining copper sulfate crystals from CuSO₄(aq)

### Simple Distillation
Separates a **solvent from a solution** (liquid from dissolved solid).
- Solution is heated; solvent boils, vapour condenses in condenser, pure solvent collected
- Thermometer reads the boiling point of the solvent
- Example: obtaining pure water from salt water

### Fractional Distillation
Separates **two or more miscible liquids** with different boiling points.
- Uses a fractionating column
- Liquid with the **lower boiling point** boils first and is collected
- Example: separating ethanol (bp 78°C) from water (bp 100°C)

### Paper Chromatography
Separates **mixtures of soluble substances** based on their different solubilities.
- Sample spots placed on a pencil baseline on chromatography paper
- Paper dipped in solvent (solvent level BELOW baseline)
- Solvent rises by capillary action; components travel different distances
- Rf value = distance moved by spot / distance moved by solvent front

### Tests for Purity
- **Pure substance**: melts/boils at a single, sharp temperature
- **Impure substance**: melts/boils over a range of temperatures, and at a lower melting point / higher boiling point than the pure substance

## Key Facts

| Method | Separates | Based on |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Filtration | Insoluble solid from liquid | Particle size |
| Crystallisation | Dissolved solid from solution | Solubility changes |
| Simple distillation | Solvent from solution | Boiling point difference |
| Fractional distillation | Two miscible liquids | Boiling point difference |
| Chromatography | Soluble substances | Different solubilities in solvent |
