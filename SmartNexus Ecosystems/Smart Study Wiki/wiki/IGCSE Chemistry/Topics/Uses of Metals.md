# Uses of Metals

**Summary**: Metal uses are determined by their properties. Al: aircraft (low density). Cu: wiring (conductivity). Fe/steel: construction. Alloys improve properties over pure metals.

## Aluminium

| Property | Related Use |
|----------|-------------|
| Low density | Aircraft, drinks cans, foil |
| Corrosion resistant (Al₂O₃ layer) | Window frames, cooking utensils |
| Good electrical conductor | Power cables (with steel core for strength) |
| Malleable/ductile | Foil, shaped containers |

## Copper

| Property | Related Use |
|----------|-------------|
| Excellent electrical conductor | Electrical wiring |
| Malleable/ductile | Pipes, wires |
| Corrosion resistant | Water pipes, roofing |
| Good thermal conductor | Cooking pans, heat exchangers |

## Iron / Steel

| Type | Composition | Properties | Uses |
|------|------------|------------|------|
| Cast iron | ~4% C | Hard, brittle | Manhole covers, engine blocks |
| Mild steel | ~0.2% C | Malleable, strong | Car bodies, girders, ships |
| Stainless steel | Fe + Cr + Ni | Corrosion resistant | Cutlery, kitchen sinks, surgical tools |
| High carbon steel | ~1% C | Hard, wear-resistant | Cutting tools, drills |

## Alloys

An **alloy** is a mixture of a metal with one or more other elements (usually metals).

### Why Are Alloys Stronger Than Pure Metals?
- Pure metal: regular layers of atoms slide over each other easily
- Alloy: different-sized atoms disrupt the regular arrangement → layers cannot slide as easily

| Alloy | Composition | Properties | Uses |
|-------|------------|------------|------|
| Brass | Cu + Zn | Harder than Cu, corrosion resistant | Musical instruments, door handles |
| Bronze | Cu + Sn | Hard, resistant to corrosion | Statues, coins, ship propellers |
| Solder | Pb + Sn | Low melting point | Joining electrical components |
| Duralumin | Al + Cu + Mg | Low density, very strong | Aircraft bodies |
| Steel | Fe + C + others | Strong, versatile | Construction, vehicles |

## Key Facts
- Metal uses are ALWAYS linked to specific properties
- "Explain why [metal] is used for [purpose]" → state the property FIRST, then link to the use
- Alloys are harder/stronger than pure metals because different atom sizes prevent layers sliding
- Stainless steel = Fe + Cr + Ni (corrosion resistant and hard)
