# Alcohols

**Summary**: Contain —OH functional group: CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH. Ethanol made by fermentation (glucose → ethanol + CO₂) or hydration of ethene (C₂H₄ + H₂O).

## Functional Group: —OH (Hydroxyl)

General formula: CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH

| Alcohol | Formula | Boiling point |
|---------|---------|--------------|
| Methanol | CH₃OH | 65°C |
| Ethanol | C₂H₅OH | 78°C |
| Propanol | C₃H₇OH | 97°C |

## Making Ethanol

### Method 1: Fermentation
C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂

**Conditions:**
- **Yeast** (provides enzymes/zymase)
- **Anaerobic** (no oxygen — or yeast respires ethanol to CO₂)
- **30–35°C** (optimum for yeast enzymes; above 40°C enzymes denature)
- pH ~7 (neutral)

**Advantages:** Renewable (glucose from plants), low tech, low cost
**Disadvantages:** Slow, produces only ~15% ethanol (yeast die above), needs distillation

### Method 2: Hydration of Ethene
C₂H₄ + H₂O → C₂H₅OH

**Conditions:**
- **H₃PO₄** (phosphoric acid catalyst)
- **300°C**
- **60 atm**

**Advantages:** Fast, continuous process, pure product
**Disadvantages:** Non-renewable (ethene from crude oil), high energy/equipment cost

## Reactions

### Combustion
C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O
- Ethanol burns with a clean blue flame
- Used as a biofuel

### Oxidation
Ethanol → Ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH)
- Oxidised by: acidified potassium manganate(VII) (purple → colourless) or acidified potassium dichromate(VI) (orange → green)
- Or by bacteria in air (wine → vinegar)

## Key Facts
- Fermentation = YEAST + ANAEROBIC + ~35°C
- Hydration of ethene = H₃PO₄ + 300°C + 60 atm
- Fermentation is RENEWABLE; hydration is NON-RENEWABLE
- Alcohols burn in air → CO₂ + H₂O — they are good fuels
