# Reversible Reactions

**Summary**: Reactions that can go both forwards and backwards, reaching dynamic equilibrium when the rates of forward and reverse reactions are equal.

## Dynamic Equilibrium

A reversible reaction reaches **dynamic equilibrium** in a closed system when:
- The **rate of the forward reaction = rate of the backward reaction**
- The **concentrations of reactants and products remain constant** (but are NOT necessarily equal)
- Both reactions continue to occur (dynamic, not static)

## Le Chatelier's Principle

**If a system at equilibrium is subjected to a change in conditions, the position of equilibrium shifts to oppose (counteract) the change.**

### Effect of Temperature
- **Increase temperature**: Equilibrium shifts in the **endothermic** direction (absorbs the added heat)
- **Decrease temperature**: Equilibrium shifts in the **exothermic** direction (releases heat)

### Effect of Pressure (gases only)
- **Increase pressure**: Equilibrium shifts to the side with **fewer gas molecules** (reduces pressure)
- **Decrease pressure**: Equilibrium shifts to the side with **more gas molecules**

### Effect of Concentration
- **Add more reactant**: Equilibrium shifts to make more **product** (uses up added reactant)
- **Remove product**: Equilibrium shifts to make more **product** (replaces what was removed)

### Effect of Catalyst
- A catalyst speeds up BOTH forward and backward reactions equally
- A catalyst does **NOT** change the position of equilibrium
- It only helps equilibrium be reached **faster**

## The Haber Process

N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g)   ΔH = −92 kJ/mol (exothermic)

**Conditions chosen:**
- **Temperature: 450°C** — compromise: low temp favours NH₃ yield (exothermic), but high temp needed for reasonable rate
- **Pressure: 200 atm** — high pressure favours fewer molecules (4 → 2), but higher pressure is expensive/dangerous
- **Catalyst: Iron** — speeds up the reaction

## The Contact Process

2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2SO₃(g)   ΔH = −197 kJ/mol (exothermic)

**Conditions:**
- **Temperature: 450°C** — compromise between yield and rate
- **Pressure: ~2 atm** — equilibrium already favours products at low pressure
- **Catalyst: Vanadium(V) oxide (V₂O₅)**

## Key Facts
- Equilibrium only occurs in a CLOSED system
- At equilibrium, macroscopic properties are constant but reactions continue at the molecular level
- Le Chatelier: the system "does the opposite" of whatever change is imposed
- Catalysts have NO effect on equilibrium position
