# Air Pollution

**Summary**: Pollutants from fossil fuel combustion: CO (toxic), SO₂ (acid rain), NOx (acid rain, smog), particulates. Catalytic converters reduce emissions.

## Main Air Pollutants

| Pollutant | Source | Effects |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Carbon monoxide (CO) | Incomplete combustion of fossil fuels in car engines | **Toxic**: binds to haemoglobin, reducing oxygen transport |
| Sulfur dioxide (SO₂) | Burning fossil fuels containing sulfur impurities (coal, diesel) | **Acid rain**: damages buildings, kills aquatic life, leaches soil nutrients |
| Nitrogen oxides (NO, NO₂ — "NOx") | Reaction of N₂ + O₂ at high temperatures in car engines | **Acid rain**, **photochemical smog**, respiratory irritation |
| Particulates / soot (C) | Incomplete combustion, diesel engines | **Respiratory problems**, global dimming |
| Unburnt hydrocarbons | Incomplete combustion, evaporation from fuel tanks | Contribute to **photochemical smog** |
| Lead compounds | Leaded petrol (now largely banned) | **Neurotoxic**, especially harmful to children |

## Acid Rain

### Formation
1. SO₂ dissolves in rainwater: SO₂ + H₂O → H₂SO₃ (sulfurous acid)
2. Further oxidation: 2SO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 2H₂SO₄ (sulfuric acid)
3. NO₂ dissolves: 2NO₂ + H₂O → HNO₂ + HNO₃ (nitrous + nitric acids)

### Effects
- Acidifies lakes and rivers → kills fish and aquatic life
- Leaches Al³⁺ from soil → damages tree roots
- Dissolves limestone/marble buildings: CaCO₃ + H₂SO₄ → CaSO₄ + H₂O + CO₂
- Corrodes metal structures

## Catalytic Converter

Fitted to car exhausts to reduce emissions:
- **2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂** (CO oxidised)
- **2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂** (NO reduced, CO oxidised)
- Contains **platinum, palladium, and rhodium** catalysts on a ceramic honeycomb
- Only works when the engine is HOT

## Reducing Pollution

- **Flue gas desulfurisation**: SO₂ removed by reacting with CaO/CaCO₃ (wet scrubbing)
- **Low-sulfur fuels**: Use natural gas instead of coal
- **Renewable energy**: Solar, wind, hydro — no combustion
- **Electric vehicles**: No tailpipe emissions

## Key Facts
- CO is toxic because it binds to haemoglobin instead of oxygen
- SO₂ and NOx cause acid rain
- Catalytic converters convert CO, NOx, and unburnt hydrocarbons to CO₂, N₂, and H₂O
- Acid rain dissolves limestone buildings and kills aquatic life
