# Redox Reactions

**Summary**: Oxidation is loss of electrons; reduction is gain of electrons (OIL RIG). Redox reactions always involve both processes occurring simultaneously.

**Created**: 2026-07-14

## Definitions

### In Terms of Oxygen (earlier definition)
- **Oxidation**: Gain of oxygen
- **Reduction**: Loss of oxygen

### In Terms of Electrons (IGCSE key definition)
- **Oxidation**: Loss of electrons
- **Reduction**: Gain of electrons

**Mnemonic: OIL RIG** — Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)

### In Terms of Oxidation Number
- **Oxidation**: Increase in oxidation number
- **Reduction**: Decrease in oxidation number

## Identifying Redox Reactions

A reaction is redox if:
- One substance is oxidised AND another is reduced simultaneously
- There is a transfer of electrons between species

### Oxidising and Reducing Agents
- **Oxidising agent**: The substance that accepts electrons (is itself reduced)
- **Reducing agent**: The substance that donates electrons (is itself oxidised)

## Examples

### Displacement Reaction
Zn(s) + CuSO₄(aq) → ZnSO₄(aq) + Cu(s)
- Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ (oxidation — Zn loses electrons)
- Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu (reduction — Cu²⁺ gains electrons)
- Zn is the reducing agent; Cu²⁺ is the oxidising agent

### Electrolysis
At the cathode: Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na (reduction — gains electron)
At the anode: 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻ (oxidation — loses electrons)

### Rusting of Iron
4Fe + 3O₂ + 2H₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·H₂O
- Fe → Fe³⁺ + 3e⁻ (oxidation)
- O₂ + 4e⁻ → 2O²⁻ (reduction)

## Common Oxidising and Reducing Agents

| Oxidising Agents | Reducing Agents |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Oxygen (O₂) | Hydrogen (H₂) |
| Chlorine (Cl₂) | Carbon (C) |
| Potassium manganate(VII) | Carbon monoxide (CO) |
| Potassium dichromate(VI) | Metals (especially reactive ones) |
| Hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) | |
| Concentrated HNO₃ | |

## Key Facts
- Oxidation and reduction ALWAYS occur together
- OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain
- A substance that is oxidised is the REDUCING agent
- A substance that is reduced is the OXIDISING agent
