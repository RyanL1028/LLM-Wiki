## Gas Tests

| Gas | Test | Positive Result |
|-----|------|----------------|
| H₂ | Lighted splint | Squeaky pop (hydrogen burns) |
| O₂ | Glowing splint | Splint relights |
| CO₂ | Bubble through limewater | Limewater turns milky/cloudy (CaCO₃ formed) |
| Cl₂ | Damp blue litmus paper | Turns red, then bleaches white |
| NH₃ | Damp red litmus paper | Turns blue |
| SO₂ | Acidified K₂Cr₂O₇ / KMnO₄ paper | Orange → green / Purple → colourless |

## Cation Tests — Flame Tests

| Metal ion | Flame colour |
|-----------|-------------|
| Li⁺ | Red / crimson |
| Na⁺ | Yellow (intense, persistent) |
| K⁺ | Lilac (pale purple) |
| Ca²⁺ | Orange-red / brick red |
| Cu²⁺ | Blue-green |
| Ba²⁺ | Apple green |
| Mg²⁺ | No colour |

**Method**: Clean nichrome wire loop in conc. HCl, dip in sample, hold in blue Bunsen flame edge.

## Cation Tests — NaOH / NH₃ Precipitation

| Ion | NaOH(aq) — small amount | NaOH(aq) — excess | NH₃(aq) |
|-----|------------------------|-------------------|---------|
| Ca²⁺ | White ppt | Insoluble | No ppt / v. slight |
| Mg²⁺ | White ppt | Insoluble | White ppt |
| Al³⁺ | White ppt | **Dissolves** (colourless) | White ppt (insoluble) |
| Zn²⁺ | White ppt | **Dissolves** (colourless) | White ppt, **dissolves in excess** |
| Fe²⁺ | Green ppt | Insoluble | Green ppt |
| Fe³⁺ | Red-brown ppt | Insoluble | Red-brown ppt |
| Cu²⁺ | Blue ppt | Insoluble | Blue ppt, **dissolves in excess → deep blue** |
| NH₄⁺ | NH₃ gas on warming | — | — (test gas with red litmus) |

## Anion Tests

| Anion | Test Reagent | Positive Result |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Cl⁻ | Acidify with HNO₃, add AgNO₃(aq) | White ppt (AgCl) |
| Br⁻ | Acidify with HNO₃, add AgNO₃(aq) | Cream ppt (AgBr) |
| I⁻ | Acidify with HNO₃, add AgNO₃(aq) | Yellow ppt (AgI) |
| SO₄²⁻ | Acidify with HCl, add BaCl₂(aq) | White ppt (BaSO₄) |
| CO₃²⁻ | Add dilute acid | Effervescence; gas turns limewater milky |
| NO₃⁻ | Add NaOH(aq) + Al foil, warm gently | NH₃ gas evolved (red litmus → blue) |

## Test for Water
- **Anhydrous CuSO₄**: White → Blue
- **Anhydrous CoCl₂**: Blue → Pink
- **Boiling point test**: Pure water boils at exactly 100°C

## Key Facts
- Always acidify with HNO₃ before halide test (removes CO₃²⁻)
- Always acidify with HCl before sulfate test (removes CO₃²⁻/SO₃²⁻)
- Amphoteric hydroxides: Al(OH)₃ and Zn(OH)₂ dissolve in excess NaOH
- Na⁺ and K⁺ are identified by flame test ONLY (no precipitate with NaOH)
