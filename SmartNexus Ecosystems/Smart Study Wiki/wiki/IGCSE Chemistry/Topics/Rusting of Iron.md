# Rusting of Iron

**Summary**: Rusting requires oxygen AND water. Prevention: barrier (paint, oil, plastic), sacrificial protection (galvanising with zinc), and alloying (stainless steel).

## What is Rust?

Rust = hydrated iron(III) oxide (Fe₂O₃·xH₂O). It is a flaky, porous, reddish-brown solid that does NOT protect the underlying iron (unlike Al₂O₃ on aluminium).

## Conditions for Rusting

**Both OXYGEN and WATER are required.** If either is absent, rusting does not occur.

- Iron nail in dry air (CaCl₂ desiccant) → no rust
- Iron nail in boiled water (no dissolved O₂) under oil layer → no rust
- Iron nail in water exposed to air → rust forms

## Factors That Accelerate Rusting
- **Salt** (NaCl) — e.g., coastal areas, road salt
- **Acidic conditions** — acid rain, industrial pollution
- **Higher temperature** — faster reaction rate

## Prevention Methods

### 1. Barrier Protection
Prevents oxygen and water from reaching the iron surface.
- **Paint** — bridges, cars, ships
- **Oil/grease** — moving parts, tools, machinery
- **Plastic coating** — wire racks, garden furniture
- **Tin plating** ("tin" cans) — BUT if scratched, iron rusts FASTER because Fe is more reactive than Sn (Fe acts as a sacrificial anode to Sn)
- **Chrome plating** — decorative, corrosion-resistant

### 2. Sacrificial Protection
A **more reactive metal** is attached to the iron. The more reactive metal corrodes INSTEAD of the iron.

- **Galvanising** — coating iron with ZINC
  - Even if scratched, zinc reacts preferentially (Zn is more reactive than Fe)
  - Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ (zinc oxidises instead of iron)
- **Magnesium blocks** — attached to ship hulls and underground pipelines

### 3. Alloying
- **Stainless steel** (Fe + Cr + Ni) — chromium forms a protective Cr₂O₃ layer
- More expensive but permanent protection

## Key Facts
- Rust = hydrated iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃·xH₂O
- BOTH oxygen AND water are essential for rusting
- Salt and acid accelerate rusting
- Sacrificial protection: use a MORE reactive metal (Zn, Mg) that corrodes instead of Fe
- Galvanising = coating with zinc — protection even when scratched
