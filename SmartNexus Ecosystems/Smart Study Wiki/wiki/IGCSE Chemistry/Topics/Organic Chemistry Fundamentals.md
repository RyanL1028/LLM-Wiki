## What is Organic Chemistry?

The study of **carbon compounds** (except CO, CO₂, carbonates, and cyanides which are inorganic). All organic compounds contain carbon and usually hydrogen.

## Homologous Series

A **homologous series** is a family of organic compounds with:
1. The same **functional group**
2. The same **general formula**
3. A **gradual trend** in physical properties (e.g., boiling points increase with chain length)
4. **Similar chemical properties** (due to same functional group)
5. Each member differs from the next by **—CH₂—**

### Key Homologous Series at IGCSE

| Series | Functional Group | General Formula | Example |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|---------|
| Alkanes | C—C (single bonds only) | CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ | CH₄ (methane) |
| Alkenes | C=C (double bond) | CₙH₂ₙ | C₂H₄ (ethene) |
| Alcohols | —OH (hydroxyl) | CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH | C₂H₅OH (ethanol) |
| Carboxylic acids | —COOH (carboxyl) | CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH | CH₃COOH (ethanoic acid) |

## Functional Groups

A functional group is an atom or group of atoms that gives a homologous series its **characteristic chemical reactions**.

## Isomerism

**Isomers** are molecules with the **same molecular formula** but **different structural formula** (different arrangement of atoms).

- Butane (C₄H₁₀) has 2 isomers: butane and methylpropane
- Pentane (C₅H₁₂) has 3 isomers
- Ethanol (C₂H₅OH) and methoxymethane (CH₃OCH₃) are functional group isomers

## Nomenclature (Naming)

1. Identify the longest continuous carbon chain → gives the root name
2. Identify the functional group → gives the suffix or prefix
3. Number the carbons so the functional group gets the lowest number
4. Name any branches (alkyl groups)

**Prefixes:** meth- (1C), eth- (2C), prop- (3C), but- (4C), pent- (5C), hex- (6C)

## Key Facts
- All organic compounds burn in excess oxygen → CO₂ + H₂O
- Same functional group → same chemical reactions
- Molecular formula tells you the atoms; structural formula shows the arrangement
- Displayed formula shows ALL atoms and ALL bonds
