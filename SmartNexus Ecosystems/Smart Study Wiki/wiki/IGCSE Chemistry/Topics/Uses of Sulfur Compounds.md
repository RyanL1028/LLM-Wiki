# Uses of Sulfur Compounds

**Summary**: Sulfuric acid is used in fertilisers, detergents, paints, plastics, metal processing, and car batteries. SO₂ used in food preservation and bleaching.

## Uses of Sulfuric Acid (H₂SO₄)

The "king of chemicals" — most manufactured chemical worldwide.

| Use | Details |
|-----|---------|
| **Fertilisers** | Making ammonium sulfate, superphosphate, NPK fertilisers |
| **Detergents** | Sulfonation of organic compounds to make surfactants |
| **Paints and pigments** | Titanium dioxide (TiO₂) production |
| **Plastics and fibres** | Nylon, rayon production |
| **Metal processing** | Pickling (cleaning) steel before galvanising |
| **Car batteries** | Electrolyte in lead-acid batteries |
| **Drain cleaners** | Dissolves organic blockages |

## Uses of Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂)

| Use | Details |
|-----|---------|
| **Food preservative** | Kills bacteria/mould on dried fruit; prevents browning (E220) |
| **Bleaching agent** | Bleaches wood pulp for paper (different from chlorine — SO₂ reduction, reversible) |
| **Making sulfuric acid** | Via the Contact Process |
| **Sterilising equipment** | In wine/beer making |

## Uses of Sulfur Itself
- **Vulcanisation of rubber** — cross-links rubber polymers → more elastic and durable tyres
- **Fungicides and pesticides** — sulfur dust/sprays
- **Matches and gunpowder** — sulfur + KNO₃ + C
- **Pharmaceuticals** — sulfonamide antibiotics

## Key Facts
- H₂SO₄ is the "king of chemicals" — most made chemical globally
- Fertilisers are the largest use of sulfuric acid
- SO₂ is used as a food preservative (E220) — prevents browning
- Sulfur is used to vulcanise rubber
