# Metal Extraction

**Summary**: Metals above carbon → extracted by electrolysis. Metals below carbon → extracted by reduction with carbon/coke. Iron: blast furnace. Aluminium: Hall-Héroult electrolysis.

## Extraction Methods

### Metals ABOVE Carbon (K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al)
- Must be extracted by **electrolysis** of their molten compounds
- Carbon cannot reduce these metal oxides (metals are stronger reducing agents than carbon)

### Metals BELOW Carbon (Zn, Fe, Pb, Cu)
- Extracted by **reduction with carbon (coke)** in a furnace
- Carbon is a cheaper reducing agent than electricity

### Metals at the Bottom (Ag, Au)
- Found **native** (uncombined) in the Earth's crust
- Very unreactive — do not form compounds easily

## Iron Extraction — The Blast Furnace

**Raw materials:** Iron ore (haematite, Fe₂O₃), coke (C), limestone (CaCO₃)

**Process:**
1. Coke burns: C + O₂ → CO₂ (heat generated)
2. CO₂ reduced: CO₂ + C → 2CO (carbon monoxide — the reducing agent)
3. Iron oxide reduced: Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂
4. Limestone removes impurities: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂; CaO + SiO₂ → CaSiO₃ (slag)

**Products:**
- **Molten iron** — tapped from bottom, used to make steel
- **Slag (CaSiO₃)** — floats on iron, tapped separately, used for road building

## Aluminium Extraction — Hall-Héroult Process

- Aluminium oxide (Al₂O₃) from bauxite
- Dissolved in **molten cryolite** (Na₃AlF₆) — lowers melting point from ~2000°C to ~950°C
- Electrolysis: Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al at cathode; 2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻ at anode
- Graphite anodes consumed: C + O₂ → CO₂

## Zinc Extraction
- ZnO reduced with coke: ZnO + C → Zn + CO
- Zinc is volatile — it vaporises and is condensed

## Copper Extraction
- Copper ores (e.g., CuFeS₂) are concentrated, roasted, and smelted
- Impure copper is purified by electrolysis

## Key Facts
- Carbon can only reduce metal oxides of metals LESS reactive than carbon
- K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al → electrolysis; Zn, Fe, Pb, Cu → carbon reduction; Ag, Au → native
- Limestone in blast furnace removes sand (SiO₂) as slag
- Cryolite saves energy and cost in aluminium extraction
