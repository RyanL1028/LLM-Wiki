# Climate Change and Greenhouse Gases

**Summary**: Greenhouse gases (CO₂, CH₄, H₂O vapour) trap infrared radiation. Enhanced greenhouse effect → global warming. Carbon footprint measures emissions.

## The Greenhouse Effect (Natural)

1. Sun emits short-wavelength radiation (visible + UV)
2. Earth absorbs this and re-emits as long-wavelength **infrared (IR) radiation**
3. **Greenhouse gases** in the atmosphere absorb IR radiation
4. This trapped energy warms the Earth
5. Without it, Earth would be ~33°C colder — the greenhouse effect is ESSENTIAL for life

## The Enhanced Greenhouse Effect (Problem)

Human activities have **increased** greenhouse gas concentrations → more IR trapped → Earth warms more = **global warming / climate change**.

## Key Greenhouse Gases

| Gas | Sources | Relative effect |
|-----|---------|----------------|
| Carbon dioxide (CO₂) | Burning fossil fuels, deforestation, cement production | Reference (GWP = 1) |
| Methane (CH₄) | Cattle farming, rice paddies, landfill decomposition, natural gas leaks | ~25× more potent than CO₂ |
| Water vapour (H₂O) | Natural evaporation (amplifies warming — feedback effect) | Most abundant |
| Nitrous oxide (N₂O) | Fertilisers, industrial processes | ~300× more potent than CO₂ |
| CFCs | Refrigerants (now banned by Montreal Protocol) | Very high GWP |

## Effects of Climate Change

- Rising global temperatures
- Melting ice caps and glaciers → sea level rise
- More frequent extreme weather (floods, droughts, storms)
- Changes to ecosystems and agriculture
- Ocean acidification (CO₂ dissolving in oceans)

## Carbon Footprint

The **total amount of CO₂ and other greenhouse gases** emitted over the full life cycle of a product, service, or event.

**Ways to reduce carbon footprint:**
- Use renewable energy (solar, wind)
- Improve energy efficiency
- Carbon capture and storage
- Plant trees (reforestation)
- Reduce, reuse, recycle

## Key Facts
- Greenhouse gases absorb IR radiation, not UV/visible
- CO₂ is the main anthropogenic (human-caused) greenhouse gas
- Methane is ~25× more potent than CO₂ per molecule
- Carbon footprint includes ALL emissions throughout a product's life cycle
