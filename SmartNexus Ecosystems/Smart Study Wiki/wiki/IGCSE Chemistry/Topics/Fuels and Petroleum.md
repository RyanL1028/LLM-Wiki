## Crude Oil (Petroleum)

A mixture of hydrocarbons (mostly alkanes), formed from ancient marine organisms over millions of years. It is a **non-renewable** resource.

## Fractional Distillation

Crude oil is separated into fractions based on **boiling points**.

### Process:
1. Crude oil is heated to ~350°C in a furnace
2. Vapours enter the fractionating column (hot at bottom, cooler at top)
3. Vapours rise and condense at different heights (at their boiling points)
4. Fractions with higher boiling points condense lower down; lower boiling points condense higher up

### Fractions (lightest → heaviest):

| Fraction | Carbon range | Boiling range | Uses |
|----------|-------------|---------------|------|
| Refinery gas | C₁–C₄ | <25°C | Bottled gas, heating |
| Petrol (gasoline) | C₅–C₁₀ | 25–175°C | Car fuel |
| Naphtha | C₇–C₁₂ | 100–200°C | Chemical feedstock (cracking) |
| Kerosene/paraffin | C₁₀–C₁₆ | 175–275°C | Jet fuel, heating |
| Diesel/gas oil | C₁₄–C₂₀ | 250–350°C | Diesel engines |
| Fuel oil | C₂₀–C₅₀ | 350–450°C | Ships, power stations |
| Bitumen | >C₅₀ | >450°C | Road surfacing, roofing |

### Trends Down the Column
- Chain length **increases**
- Boiling point **increases**
- Viscosity **increases**
- Flammability **decreases**
- Colour darkens

## Cracking

Breaking long-chain alkanes into shorter, more useful alkanes + alkenes.

**Why crack?**
- More demand for petrol (C₅–C₁₀) than heavy fractions
- Alkenes are needed for polymers and chemicals

**Conditions:**
- **Catalytic cracking**: Al₂O₃ (alumina) catalyst, ~550°C
- **Thermal cracking**: High temperature (~700°C) and pressure

## Biofuels

**Renewable** alternatives to fossil fuels:
- **Bioethanol**: Made by fermentation of sugars/starch from plants
- **Biodiesel**: Made from vegetable oils by transesterification
- **Biogas**: Methane from anaerobic digestion of waste

## Key Facts
- Crude oil is a NON-RENEWABLE mixture of hydrocarbons
- Fractions separated by boiling point in the fractionating column
- Smaller molecules = lower boiling point = more flammable = less viscous
- Cracking: long alkanes → shorter alkanes + alkenes
