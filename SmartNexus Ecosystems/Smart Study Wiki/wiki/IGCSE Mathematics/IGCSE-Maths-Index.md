### 1. Number

- [[Number]] — Number types, HCF and LCM, fractions/decimals/percentages, ratio and proportion, standard form, surds, upper and lower bounds, compound measures, set notation and Venn diagrams

### 2. Algebra and Graphs

- [[Algebra and Graphs]] — Expanding and factorising, linear and quadratic equations, simultaneous equations, inequalities, sequences, functions, straight-line graphs (gradient, midpoint, distance, parallel/perpendicular lines), graphs of functions, gradients of curves

### 3. Geometry

- [[Geometry]] — Angle facts, triangles and polygons, circle theorems, symmetry, similarity and congruence

### 4. Mensuration

- [[Mensuration]] — Perimeter and area of 2D shapes, arc length and sector area, surface area and volume of 3D shapes, units conversion

### 5. Trigonometry

- [[Trigonometry]] — SOHCAHTOA, exact trigonometric values, sine and cosine rules, area of a triangle (1/2 ab sin C), 3D trigonometry, bearings

### 6. Vectors and Transformations

- [[Vectors and Transformations]] — Vector notation and operations, vector geometry, transformations (reflection, rotation, translation, enlargement), matrices for transformations, combined transformations

### 7. Probability

- [[Probability]] — Basic probability, sample spaces, mutually exclusive and independent events, tree diagrams, conditional probability, Venn diagrams for probability, expected frequency

### 8. Statistics

- [[Statistics]] — Data types, averages and measures of spread, statistical charts (bar, pie, stem-and-leaf, histogram, cumulative frequency, box-and-whisker), scatter graphs and correlation
