> **Key idea**
> More than one force can act on an object. [[Forces]] are balanced when they are the same size and act in opposite directions. Forces are unbalanced when they are not the same size.

> **Key words**
> balanced, equilibrium, unbalanced, driving force, resistive force

**When you wake up in the morning, you need a force to get out of bed and moving.**

### What are balanced forces?

When the forces acting on an object are the same size but act in opposite directions, they are **balanced**. You can think of balanced forces as two teams at each end of a rope, pulling. If each team pulls with the same force, the rope does not move because the forces cancel out. The object is in **equilibrium**.

All stationary (still) objects are in equilibrium. There has to be a support force acting on them to balance out their [[Weight]]. The person in Figure 1 is in equilibrium when they lie in bed. The support force and their weight are balanced.

### What are unbalanced forces?

Figure 2 shows a rocket-powered car. The forces acting on it are **unbalanced** – they are not the same size. This means that they do not cancel out. The **driving force** from the engine is shown in blue. The **resistive forces** from air resistance and [[Friction]] are shown in red.

![Two diagrams showing the forces acting on a rocket-powered car before and after a parachute attached to the back of the car is fired.](images/9781382063821_graphic_282.svg)

*Figure 2The car will change [[Speed]] depending on what direction the unbalanced force is in.  Full text alternative to the content*

### How do unbalanced forces cause changes in speed?

When the car’s rocket-powered engine starts moving, the driving force will become very big very quickly and the car will speed up. When the driver wants to stop, they will fire a parachute to slow the car down. In both cases, the forces acting on the car are unbalanced.

The driver uses a parachute to slow the car down because it creates a much bigger resistive force than using the brakes alone. This means the car will slow down much faster and stop in a much shorter time.

### How do unbalanced forces cause changes in direction?

Isaac Newton worked out that Earth exerts a force on the Moon. The force of [[Gravity]] acting on the Moon keeps the Moon in orbit (on a path) around Earth, as you can see in Figure 3. The force that keeps the Moon orbiting Earth is the same force that acts on an apple and pulls it to the ground. It changes the direction of motion, but not the speed.

![An illustration showing the moon revolving around Earth in a fixed circular orbit.](images/9781382063821_graphic_284.svg)

*Figure 3The force of gravity keeps the Moon in orbit around Earth.*

Just like the Moon orbiting Earth, every time you go around a corner in a car, the friction between the tyres and the road changes the direction of the car.

> **Working scientifically**
> Isaac Newton said, ‘For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction’. The forces in an interaction pair are equal and opposite. Is lying in bed an example of this law? No, it is not. Each of the forces acting on you comes from a *different* interaction pair.
