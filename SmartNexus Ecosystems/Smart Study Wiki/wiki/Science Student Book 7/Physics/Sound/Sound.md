> **In this chapter, you will:**
> - learn about [[Sound]], how it is produced, how it travels, and its [[Speed]]
> - discover how the wave properties of sound explain how we hear
> - study some of the ways that we can use sound and ultrasound to help us in our daily lives.

> **Think back**
> 1. Name two objects in your house that produce sound.
> 2. Name the part of the human body that detects (recognizes) sound.
> 3. Describe two ways that sounds made on a guitar can be different from each other.

> **Key ideas**
> Vibrating sources produce sound.
> Sounds have pitch that can be high or low, and loudness that can be loud or soft.
> Sound can travel through materials, like the air, water, and the ground.
