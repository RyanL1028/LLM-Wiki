> **In this chapter, you have:**
> - learned about [[Sound]] wave properties – waves have a wavelength, a frequency, and an amplitude, and they travel at different speeds
> - understood that vibrating objects produce sound, which travels fastest in solids because the particles are closer together
> - learned that sound cannot travel through a vacuum
> - studied how the ear detects sound to produce a signal that is sent to the brain, and that a microphone does a similar thing
> - discovered how we can use echoes to measure distance and to produce ultrasound images
> - learned that we can use sound for lots of different things, from playing instruments and listening to music, to looking inside the human body and treating patients in hospital.
