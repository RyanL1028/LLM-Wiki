> **In this chapter, you have:**
> - learned how [[Motion]] graphs show how the [[Speed]] of an object changes with time
> - used distance and time to calculate speed when it is constant, or calculated speed using a motion graph
> - used the particle model to explain gas [[Pressure]]
> - learned that gas pressure increases with increased temperature and decreased volume
> - learned that atmospheric pressure and liquid pressure both increase as you go ʻdeeperʼ
> - calculated the pressure on a solid surface using force and area
> - calculated the moment (or turning effect) of a force around a pivot using force and distance
> - found out that if an object is balanced, the moments about a pivot are equal.
