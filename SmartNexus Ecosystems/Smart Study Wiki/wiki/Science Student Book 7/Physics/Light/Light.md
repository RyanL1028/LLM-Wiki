# 3 Light

![](images/9781382063821_graphic_370.jpg)
> **In this chapter, you will:**
> - learn about [[Light]], where it comes from, and how fast it travels
> - find out how light can be reflected and change direction
> - follow the journey light takes from a source to the eye or a camera
> - study the colours of light, filters, and why objects appear different colours.

> **Think back**
> 1. Name one transparent object, one translucent object, and one opaque object.
> 2. Describe a situation when you can see your own shadow.
> 3. Describe how you can see a cat on a street in the daytime.

> **Key ideas**
> Some objects emit (give out) light.
> Light can travel through some objects. They are transparent. Objects that light cannot travel through are opaque. They form shadows.
> Light travels in straight lines.
