# 4 Plants

![](images/9781382063821_graphic_164.jpg)
> **In this chapter, you will:**
> - learn how plants make food through the process of [[Photosynthesis]], and why photosynthesis is important for all life on Earth
> - look at how leaves are adapted for photosynthesis to take place
> - study the effects of minerals on plant growth
> - learn how plants reproduce and study the structure of flowers, pollination, fertilization, and germination
> - investigate the different methods a plant uses to disperse seeds.

> **Think back**
> 1. Draw and label a diagram of a leaf cell or a root hair cell.
> 2. Draw an animal cell. Label the parts and describe their function.
> 3. Use a table to compare the different parts of inhaled and exhaled air.

> **Key ideas**
> The shape and structure of a specialized cell means it can perform a particular function.
> Photosynthesis takes place inside chloroplasts.
> Flowers are made of a number of parts.
> Seed dispersal is the spreading out of seeds from the parent plant.
