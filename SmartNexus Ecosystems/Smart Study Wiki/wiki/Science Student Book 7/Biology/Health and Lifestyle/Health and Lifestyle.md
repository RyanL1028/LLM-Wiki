# 3 Health and lifestyle

![](images/9781382063821_graphic_145.jpg)
> **In this chapter, you will:**
> - learn about the parts of a balanced diet
> - discover how much of each part you should eat to stay healthy
> - test a range of foods to identify which nutrients they contain
> - study the main organs in the digestive system
> - learn about the role bacteria and enzymes play in digestion
> - look at the effects of harmful substances on the body.

> **Think back**
> 1. Draw a healthy meal on a plate. Label the food group for each type of food.
> 2. Name some organs in the digestive system. Add a note describing what they do.
> 3. Make a list of some harmful substances that we should not put inside our body.

> **Key ideas**
> A healthy diet contains lots of fruit and vegetables, and only a small amount of fat and sugar.
> An organ system is a group of different organs that work together to perform a certain function.
> Glucose and oxygen diffuse into [[Cells]] from the blood.
> People can take medicines when they are unwell to make them feel better.
