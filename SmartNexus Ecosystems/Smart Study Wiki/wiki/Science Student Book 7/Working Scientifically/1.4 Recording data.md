### Calculating a mean

The **mean** is a type of average. To calculate the mean, you add up all the results and divide by the number of results.

You should check your data for an **anomaly**. An anomaly is a result that is very different from the others. You should repeat the measurement to replace an anomaly. In Table 4, the third measurement for the 20 °C ball temperature, 15 cm, is an anomaly. Katie and Rahim repeat this result before calculating their mean – the ball bounces to 60 cm.
