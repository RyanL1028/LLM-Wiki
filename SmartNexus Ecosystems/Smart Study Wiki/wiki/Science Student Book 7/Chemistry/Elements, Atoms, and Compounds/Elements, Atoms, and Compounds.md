# 2 Elements, atoms, and compounds

![](images/9781382063821_graphic_211.jpg)
> **In this chapter, you will:**
> - learn about [[Elements]] and use an international code to identify them
> - find out about the tiny particles – [[Atoms]] – that make up elements, and how they join together in different ways to make up all the substances on Earth and in the Universe
> - discover that every material is made from atoms of one or more of the hundred or so elements that exist.

> **Think back**
> 1. Write down three properties of gold, three properties of water, and three properties of salt.
> 2. Draw an object that is made from gold. Explain why the properties of gold make it suitable for this object.
> 3. Draw a diagram showing the arrangement of 12 particles in a substance in the solid state.
> 4. Gold particles have a greater mass than silver particles. Predict which has the greater density, gold or silver.

> **Key ideas**
> Different materials have different properties.
> How materials are used depends on their properties.
> Everything is made up of particles.
> Three factors give a substance its properties: what its particles are like, how its particles are arranged, and how its particles move.
