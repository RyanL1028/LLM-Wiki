**Have you ever had stomach ache? Did you take an indigestion tablet?**

### What is a neutralization reaction?

Excess (extra) stomach acid makes your stomach hurt. If you take an indigestion tablet, there is a chemical reaction in your stomach. The chemical reaction removes the excess stomach acid, making new substances as it does so.

The chemical reaction of stomach acid with an indigestion tablet is an example of a **neutralization** reaction. In a neutralization reaction, an acid reacts with a substance that cancels it out. The pH gets closer to 7.

### Which substances neutralize acids?

A **base** is a substance that neutralizes an acid. Look at Figure 1 – bases include sodium hydroxide, calcium oxide, and copper oxide. Some bases dissolve in water. A base that dissolves is an **alkali**.

### How is neutralization useful?

Some soils are more acidic than others. Every plant has a favourite soil pH. You can see some examples in Table 2.

Anchali lives in Thailand. She has a farm and wants to grow tea. She tests the soil. Its pH is 4.5. The soil is too acidic to grow tea.

Anchali adds a base to the soil. The base neutralizes some of the acid in the soil. The soil pH increases to pH 5.0. It is now suitable for growing tea.

In some places, gases from burning coal make sulfur dioxide gas. The gas dissolves in rainwater to make acid rain. The rain falls in lakes. This makes the lakes more acidic. Some water animals and plants cannot live in these lakes.

Environmental organizations may add bases to acid lakes. This is happening in Figure 3. The pH of the lake water increases.
