# IDPs

**Summary**: Internally Displaced Persons - displaced within their own country.
**Tags**: #concepts #humanitarian #displacement #internal

## Content

IDPs flee violence, conflict, disasters but stay within national borders. Protected by Guiding Principles on Internal Displacement.

Assistance from national governments, UN, NGOs.
