# Gravity

**Summary**: [[Gravity]] is a non-contact force pulling masses toward each other.

**Created**: 2026-04-22 13:10:06Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-22 13:10:06Z

---

## Content

Gravity acts between all objects with mass. Near Earth, gravitational field strength is about `10 N/kg`, so weight can be estimated by:

`weight (N) = mass (kg) × gravitational field strength (N/kg)`

Gravity explains falling objects, orbital motion, and why weight changes on different celestial bodies even when mass stays constant.
