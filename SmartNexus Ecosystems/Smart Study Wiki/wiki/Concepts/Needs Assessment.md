# Needs Assessment

## Content

Needs assessment is the systematic collection and analysis of data to determine the priority needs of a population affected by crisis. It informs decision-making for humanitarian response — ensuring resources reach those most in need.

Common tools include surveys, key informant interviews, focus groups, satellite imagery, and secondary data review. Assessments are often coordinated through UN clusters to avoid duplication.

Good assessments are timely, representative, and ethically conducted with informed consent.
