## Content

A [[Mixtures|mixture]] is formed when substances are combined physically rather than chemically. Each substance keeps its own properties, and proportions can change.

Unlike [[Compounds]], mixtures can often be separated using physical methods (for example, using a magnet to separate iron from sulfur in an iron-sulfur mixture).

The source contrasts iron + sulfur as a mixture (before heating) with iron sulfide as a compound (after heating and reaction).
