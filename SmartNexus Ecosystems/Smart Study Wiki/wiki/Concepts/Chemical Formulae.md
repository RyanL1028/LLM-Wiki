## Content

A chemical formula uses [[Chemical Symbols]] and subscripts to represent atom ratios in compounds. If no subscript is shown, the count is one atom.

Examples from the source:

- `H2O` means 2 hydrogen atoms and 1 oxygen atom.
- `CO2` means 1 carbon atom and 2 oxygen atoms.
- `H2SO4` means 2 hydrogen atoms, 1 sulfur atom, and 4 oxygen atoms.

Formulae provide a language-independent way for scientists to communicate composition consistently.
