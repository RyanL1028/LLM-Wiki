# Restoration

**Summary**: Process of returning something to its original state or condition.

## Content

Restoration refers to the act of returning something — whether property, ecosystems, livelihoods, or cultural heritage — to its original or a functional state. In humanitarian contexts, restoration focuses on rebuilding communities and livelihoods after conflict or disaster.

In the Chinese historical story 完璧归赵, restoration (literally "returning the jade intact") describes Lin Xiangru's successful mission to return the He Shi Bi jade to Zhao without allowing Qin to seize it — demonstrating courage, intelligence, and loyalty.
