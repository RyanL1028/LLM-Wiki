# Displacement

**Summary**: Forced migration of populations due to conflict, persecution, or disasters.
**Tags**: #concepts #humanitarian #migration #conflict
**Created**: 2026-04-23T04:03:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-23T04:03:00Z

---

## Content

Displacement occurs when people are forced to leave their homes, leading to refugees or internally displaced persons (IDPs). Causes: War, violence, natural disasters, climate change.

Impacts: Health risks, economic hardship, social disruption.

Responses: Protection, shelter, integration, repatriation.
