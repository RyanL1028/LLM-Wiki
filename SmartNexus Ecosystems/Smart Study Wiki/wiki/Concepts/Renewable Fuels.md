# Renewable Fuels

**Summary**: Sustainable energy sources that can be replenished naturally.

**Last Updated**: 2026-04-23T01:04:00Z

## Content

Renewable fuels are energy sources that can be replenished over time through natural processes. They are sustainable alternatives to fossil fuels.

For combustion, examples include biofuels like ethanol (from corn or sugarcane) and biodiesel (from vegetable oils).

Advantages: Reduce greenhouse gas emissions, renewable, less pollution.

Used in vehicles, heating, power generation.
