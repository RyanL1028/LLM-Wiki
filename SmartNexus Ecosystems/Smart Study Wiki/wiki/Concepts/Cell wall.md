# Cell wall

Plant cells, like the one in This allows them to fit together like bricks. They contain seven parts. Like animal cells, they contain a nucleus, a cell membrane, cytoplasm, and many mitochondria. They also contain three extra parts: a cell wall, a vacuole, and chloroplasts.
