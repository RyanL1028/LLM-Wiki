# Resultant Force

**Summary**: [[Resultant Force]] is the single net force equivalent to all forces acting on an object.
**Tags**: #concepts #physics #forces
**Created**: 2026-04-22 13:10:02Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-22 13:10:02Z

---

## Content

When multiple forces act on an object, they combine to form a resultant force. If opposing forces are equal, the resultant is zero (equilibrium). If not equal, the object accelerates in the direction of the larger force.

This concept links directly to [[Balanced and Unbalanced Forces]] and is used to explain accelerating vs constant-speed motion.
