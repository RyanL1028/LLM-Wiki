# Products

**Summary**: New substances formed as a result of a chemical reaction.
**Tags**: #concepts #chemistry
**Created**: 2026-05-03T00:00:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-03T00:00:00Z

---

## Content

Products are the new substances formed when a chemical reaction completes. Atoms from the reactants rearrange their bonds to create products with different chemical properties from the starting materials.

In a word equation, products appear on the right side of the arrow:
`Reactant → Product A + Product B`

Example: In `magnesium + oxygen → magnesium oxide`, magnesium oxide is the product.

Products of combustion typically include CO2 and H2O.
