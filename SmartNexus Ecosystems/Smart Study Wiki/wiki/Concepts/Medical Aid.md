## Content

Medical aid refers to the delivery of healthcare services, medicines, equipment, and trained personnel to populations affected by conflict, disaster, or poverty. It ranges from emergency trauma care and surgery to primary healthcare, vaccination campaigns, and mental health support.

Key challenges include supply chain disruptions, attacks on health facilities, and ensuring quality and safety of pharmaceuticals. Providers include MSF (Doctors Without Borders), Red Cross/Red Crescent, WHO, and local health authorities.
