## Content

Conflict resolution encompasses diplomatic, legal, and humanitarian approaches to ending hostilities and establishing lasting peace. Key mechanisms include negotiation, mediation, peacekeeping operations, international tribunals, and post-conflict reconstruction.

In humanitarian contexts, conflict resolution is essential for securing ceasefire agreements, enabling aid delivery, and protecting civilians. Success often requires coordination between UN agencies, NGOs, and diplomatic actors.
