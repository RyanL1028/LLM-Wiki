# Word Equations

**Summary**: Representations of chemical reactions using words for reactants and products.

**Created**: 2026-04-23T01:01:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-23T01:01:00Z

## Content

Word equations describe chemical reactions by listing the names of reactants (starting substances) and products (new substances formed), connected by an arrow.

They show the rearrangement of atoms without using symbols.

Examples:
- Magnesium + oxygen → magnesium oxide
- Hydrochloric acid + sodium hydroxide → sodium chloride + water

Used to represent combustion, displacement, neutralization, etc.
