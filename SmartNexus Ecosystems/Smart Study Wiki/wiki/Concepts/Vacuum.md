# Vacuum

The dolphins you see in Elephants stamp their feet when a lion comes near them. This warning sound travels through the ground to other elephants. Sound needs a medium like a solid, liquid, or gas to travel through. It cannot travel through empty space (known as a vacuum) because there are no particles to vibrate.
