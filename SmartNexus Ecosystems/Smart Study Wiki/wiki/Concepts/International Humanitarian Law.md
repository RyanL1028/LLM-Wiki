# International Humanitarian Law

**Summary**: Legal principles governing conduct in armed conflicts to protect civilians and non-combatants.

## Content

International humanitarian law (IHL), also known as the law of war, sets rules for armed conflicts to minimize suffering. Key treaties include Geneva Conventions and Additional Protocols.

Principles: Distinction between combatants and civilians, proportionality, protection of medical personnel and facilities.

Enforcement: Through national courts, international tribunals.
