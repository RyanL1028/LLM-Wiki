## Learning Flow

1. Start with [[Atoms]] and [[Elements]].
2. Use the [[Periodic Table]] and [[Chemical Symbols]] for notation.
3. Move to [[Compounds]] and [[Molecules]].
4. Compare [[Compounds]] with [[Mixtures]].
5. Read and write [[Chemical Formulae]].
6. Understand [[Chemical Reactions]] and how they change substances, distinguishing from [[Physical Changes]] and [[Changes of State]].
7. Explore types of reactions: [[Combustion]], [[Displacement Reactions]], [[Neutralization Reactions]].
8. Distinguish [[Exothermic Reactions]] from [[Endothermic Reactions]] and understand [[Energy]] transfer.
9. Learn about [[Activation Energy]] and the role of [[Catalysts]].
10. Identify [[Reactants]] and [[Products]] and write [[Word Equations]].
11. Study [[Fuels]] including [[Fossil Fuels]], [[Renewable Fuels]], and [[Biofuels]].
