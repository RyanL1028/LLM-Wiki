## Content

Healthcare in conflict regions is severely compromised by violence, displacement, and destruction of facilities. Key challenges include access to medical supplies, security for personnel, and treatment of war-related injuries.

Solutions involve international humanitarian organizations, protected medical zones, and telemedicine. Legal frameworks under Geneva Conventions protect medical workers.
