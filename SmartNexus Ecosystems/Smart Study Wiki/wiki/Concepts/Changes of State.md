# Changes of State

**Summary**: Physical transformations between solid, liquid, and gas without creating new substances.
**Tags**: #concepts #chemistry #physics
**Created**: 2026-05-03T00:00:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-03T00:00:00Z

## Content

Changes of state are physical changes where a substance transitions between solid, liquid, and gas phases. No new substances are formed — the particles remain the same, only their arrangement and energy change.

Key changes of state:
- Melting: solid → liquid
- Freezing: liquid → solid
- Boiling/Evaporating: liquid → gas
- Condensing: gas → liquid
- Sublimation: solid → gas

These are reversible and contrast with chemical reactions, which create new substances.
