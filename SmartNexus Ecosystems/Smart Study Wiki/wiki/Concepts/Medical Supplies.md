## Content

Medical supplies include drugs, vaccines, surgical tools, bandages, and diagnostic equipment needed for treatment. In conflict zones, access is hindered by disrupted supply chains, theft, and expiration due to poor storage.

Key challenges: Cold chain for vaccines, counterfeit drugs, equitable distribution.

Solutions: Pre-positioning stocks, local production, international procurement.
