## Summary

[[Foundation Model Transparency Index (FMTI)]] is presented as a framework that scores major AI providers on disclosure across indicators tied to data, safety, governance, and deployment transparency.

## Indicators Mentioned in Source

- Training data sourcing and licensing disclosure
- Safety and red-team reporting
- Governance and misuse policy clarity
- Visibility into deployment contexts and downstream use

## Findings Referenced

- Average transparency score declined year-over-year.
- Proprietary models were described as less transparent than open-weight counterparts.
- Low transparency reduces accountability and makes independent safety verification harder.
