**(b)** The table shows information about four elements in Group VII of the Periodic Table.

| Element | Atomic Number | State at room temp | Colour |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|--------|
| Fluorine | 9 | gas | pale yellow |
| Chlorine | 17 | gas | green |
| Bromine | 35 | liquid | red-brown |
| Iodine | 53 | solid | grey-black |

(i) State the trend in reactivity down Group VII. [1]

(ii) Chlorine is used to kill bacteria in water. Suggest why iodine is not used for this purpose, despite being in the same group. [1]

(iii) Name the type of bonding present in a chlorine molecule, Cl₂. [1]
