### General Marking Principles

- Marks are awarded for correct answers as shown below.
- Acceptable alternatives are separated by `/`.
- Ignore spelling errors unless the word is ambiguous.
- Where units are required, award the mark only if the unit is correct.
- Calculations: award full marks for a correct answer even without working, unless "show your working" is stated. If the answer is incorrect, award partial marks for correct steps.
