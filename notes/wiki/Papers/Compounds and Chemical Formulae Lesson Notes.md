## Summary

This lesson introduces [[Compounds]], [[Mixtures]], [[Molecules]], and [[Chemical Formulae]], then contrasts how compounds differ from their source elements in both composition and properties.

## Core Ideas

- Compounds are chemically bonded substances with fixed composition.
- Mixtures are not chemically bonded and can often be physically separated.
- Chemical formulae encode the relative number of atoms in a compound.
- Naming rules include `-ide` for simple binary compounds and common `-ate` patterns when oxygen-containing groups are present.

## Worked Examples Mentioned

- `H2O`, `CO2`, `MgO`, `NaCl`, `FeS`, `Al2O3`, `CaCO3`, `MgCl2`, `H2SO4`
- Iron + sulfur mixture vs iron sulfide compound demonstration
