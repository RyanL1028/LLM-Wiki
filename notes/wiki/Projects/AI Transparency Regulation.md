## Problem Framing

Current regulation is fragmented and often weakly enforced, while AI deployment scale continues to grow. The source highlights that voluntary commitments are insufficient for consistent transparency outcomes.

## Measures Highlighted

- Mandatory disclosure requirements (model cards, data sourcing, safety benchmarks)
- Independent third-party auditing for high-impact AI deployments
- Public registries of deployed AI systems and risk classes
- Whistleblower protections for employees surfacing AI safety failures

## Practical Checklist

- Procurement should require transparency SLAs from AI vendors.
- Organizations should maintain internal AI audit trails.
- Schools and public institutions should define AI transparency policies before rollout.
