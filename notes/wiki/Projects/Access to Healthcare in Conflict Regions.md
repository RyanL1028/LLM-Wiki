## Overview

Access to healthcare in conflict regions is a critical global issue, with millions affected by violence disrupting medical services. This project explores regulatory frameworks, international cooperation, and innovative solutions to improve healthcare delivery.

## Key Challenges

- Destruction of infrastructure and supply chains.
- Security threats to medical personnel.
- Displacement of populations and resource scarcity.
- Legal barriers under international humanitarian law.

## Proposed Solutions

- Establish protected medical corridors and zones.
- Leverage technology for remote healthcare (telemedicine).
- Strengthen coordination between UN agencies, NGOs, and governments.
- Develop sustainable funding mechanisms for conflict-zone aid.
