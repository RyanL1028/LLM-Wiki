# Physical Changes

**Summary**: Reversible changes that alter appearance or state without creating new substances.
**Tags**: #concepts #chemistry
**Created**: 2026-04-23T00:00:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-23T00:00:00Z

---

## Content

Physical changes do not rearrange atoms or create new substances. They are reversible, allowing return to original form.

Examples: melting, freezing, boiling, condensing, dissolving, changes of state.

Contrast with [[Chemical Reactions]] which form new substances and are often irreversible.
