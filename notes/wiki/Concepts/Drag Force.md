# Drag Force

**Summary**: [[Drag Force]] is resistive force from air or water acting opposite motion.
**Tags**: #concepts #physics #forces
**Created**: 2026-04-22 13:10:05Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-22 13:10:05Z

---

## Content

Drag (air resistance or water resistance) opposes movement through fluids. Increasing speed typically increases drag, and reducing drag often requires streamlining.

Examples include cyclists lowering body position, birds tucking wings, and aerodynamic vehicle design.
