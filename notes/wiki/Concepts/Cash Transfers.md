# Cash Transfers

**Summary**: Providing money directly to aid recipients for purchasing needs.
**Tags**: #concepts #humanitarian #aid #finance
**Created**: 2026-04-23T05:03:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-23T05:03:00Z

---

## Content

Cash transfers give beneficiaries money to buy essentials, allowing choice and supporting local economies. Types: unconditional, conditional.

Advantages: Dignity, efficiency, market stimulation.

Challenges: Inflation, security in cash handling.
