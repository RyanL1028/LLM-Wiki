# Contact and Non-Contact Forces

**Summary**: Contact forces require touching; non-contact forces act at a distance.
**Tags**: #concepts #physics #forces
**Created**: 2026-04-22 13:10:01Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-22 13:10:01Z

---

## Content

[[Forces]] can be classified by whether objects must touch.

- Contact forces: friction, drag/air resistance, upthrust, normal reaction.
- Non-contact forces: gravity, magnetic force, electrostatic force.

This distinction helps in free-body diagrams and in identifying force interactions in motion questions.
