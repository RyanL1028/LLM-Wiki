## Content

Surveys are a primary method of data collection in humanitarian needs assessment. They use structured questionnaires to gather quantitative and qualitative information from affected populations about their conditions, needs, and priorities.

Types include household surveys, rapid assessments, and phone-based surveys. Proper survey design ensures representative sampling, clear questions, and ethical standards including informed consent and data privacy.
