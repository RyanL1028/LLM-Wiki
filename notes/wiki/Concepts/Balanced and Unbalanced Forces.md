# Balanced and Unbalanced Forces

**Summary**: Balanced forces give zero resultant; unbalanced forces create acceleration.
**Tags**: #concepts #physics #forces
**Created**: 2026-04-22 13:10:03Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-22 13:10:03Z

---

## Content

Balanced forces are equal in size and opposite in direction, so the [[Resultant Force]] is zero. The object stays at rest or moves at constant speed in a straight line.

Unbalanced forces occur when opposing forces are not equal. The object then speeds up, slows down, or changes direction.

Typical lesson examples compare driving force with resistive forces such as [[Drag Force]] and [[Friction]].
