# Forces

## Content

Forces explain why objects move, stop, speed up, slow down, or change direction. In the source lessons, forces are measured in newtons (`N`) using a newton meter and often represented using force arrows.

Forces can be grouped as [[Contact and Non-Contact Forces|contact]] or non-contact forces. Real systems usually involve multiple forces acting at once, so analyzing the [[Resultant Force]] is important.
