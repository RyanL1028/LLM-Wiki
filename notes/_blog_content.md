# Behind NAISHK Smart Study: From Worksheets to a Full Learning Platform

When we first started building NAISHK Smart Study, the goal was simple: give students a better way to study. We had a handful of revision notes, some flashcards, and a rough idea of where we wanted to go. Today, the platform looks completely different — and we wanted to share the journey.

## It Started With Worksheets

The first big push came from one question: *how do you actually practise maths?* Watching videos and reading notes only gets you so far. Students need to work through problems.

We brought in over 110 DrFrost Year 7 maths worksheets — covering fractions, algebra, number theory, transformations, rounding, and more. Each worksheet was cleaned and uploaded with proper subject tagging, so you can filter by topic and find exactly what you need in seconds.

But worksheets alone aren't enough. You need to know what you're getting wrong, and why.

## AI-Powered Exam Marking

We built a custom exam engine that does something most platforms don't: **AI-powered auto-grading with keyword matching**. You type your answer, and the system checks it against expected keywords — not just exact matches, but intelligent grouping that understands that "gravitational force" and "gravity" are the same thing.

We wrote five full Year 7 maths papers covering fractions, algebra, number theory, transformations, and rounding. Each question is tagged to a specific topic, which feeds into something even more powerful: **Study Analytics**.

## Study Analytics: Know Where You Stand

The Analytics dashboard (find it under the Smart Study dropdown) breaks down your exam performance by topic. It shows you:

- Your overall score and number of papers taken
- Per-subject breakdowns with visual bar charts
- Topic-by-topic scores, colour-coded from red (needs work) to green (mastered)
- **Personalised worksheet recommendations** for your weakest topics

No more guessing what to study next. The platform tells you.

## Resources, Rebuilt

We completely overhauled the Resources page. You can now:

- **Search** by name across all worksheets
- **Preview PDFs inline** before downloading — click Preview to open in a new tab
- **Filter by subject** using the All Resources / Maths Resources tabs
- Everything loads fast, with resources chunked behind the scenes for smooth delivery

## AI Tools Suite

Beyond exams and worksheets, we built a set of standalone AI tools:

- **AI Mark** — get instant feedback on past paper answers with detailed marking
- **Flashcards** — upload any document and generate study flashcards automatically
- **Summary** — upload a file and get a concise summary
- **Essay Review** — upload an essay (.docx or .pdf) for AI-powered feedback on structure, argument quality, and areas to improve

Each AI tool sits under the AI Tools dropdown, available to all members.

## The Wiki: Concepts and Papers

We built out a structured wiki with dedicated sections for **Concepts** (over 90 entries spanning chemistry, physics, maths, international relations, and humanitarian aid) and **Papers** (research paper summaries and lesson notes). Every entry links to related topics, building a knowledge graph you can navigate naturally.

## What's Next?

We're not close to done. Upcoming features we're exploring include more subjects for worksheets, interactive quizzes, and deeper analytics.

NAISHK Smart Study has grown from a simple revision helper into a full learning platform. If you haven't checked out the latest features, log in and explore — and as always, if you find a bug or have an idea, send us a support ticket.
