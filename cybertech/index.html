Who We Are

Student-Made, Student-Driven

Founded in 2025 at NAISHK — one student, one laptop, one idea that grew into something bigger.

🏫

NAISHK Roots

Born in a Hong Kong secondary school, CyberTech builds tools that solve real problems faced by students and teachers every day.

🛠

Built With

Firebase, vanilla JavaScript, HTML/CSS, the WhatsApp Cloud API, the Base44 AI platform, and a lot of late nights.

Privacy First
