# Chemical Reactions

**Summary**: Processes where atoms rearrange to form new substances with different properties.
**Tags**: #concepts #chemistry
**Created**: 2026-04-23T00:00:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-23T00:00:00Z

---

## Content

A chemical reaction is a change where atoms are rearranged to create new substances. Elements and compounds participate, producing new materials with transferred energy. Most are irreversible.

Evidence: gas release, temperature change, color change, flames, smells, sounds.

Types:
- Exothermic: releases energy (feels hot)
- Endothermic: absorbs energy (feels cold)
- Displacement: more reactive metal replaces less reactive
- Neutralization: acid + alkali → salt + water

Catalysts speed up reactions without being used up.

Contrast with [[Physical Changes]] which are reversible and don't form new substances.
