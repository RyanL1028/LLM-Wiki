# Mass and Weight

**Summary**: [[Mass and Weight]] are different: mass is matter amount, weight is gravitational force.
**Tags**: #concepts #physics #forces
**Created**: 2026-04-22 13:10:07Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-22 13:10:07Z

---

## Content

Mass is measured in kilograms (`kg`) and does not change with location. Weight is measured in newtons (`N`) and depends on local gravitational field strength.

The relationship is:

`weight = mass × gravitational field strength`

This explains why someone has the same mass on Earth and Moon but lower weight on the Moon.
