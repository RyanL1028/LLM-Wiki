# Reactants

**Summary**: Starting substances in a chemical reaction that are transformed into products.
**Tags**: #concepts #chemistry
**Created**: 2026-05-03T00:00:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-03T00:00:00Z

---

## Content

Reactants are the starting substances present before a chemical reaction takes place. During a reaction, bonds between atoms in reactants break and rearrange to form new substances called products.

In a word equation, reactants appear on the left side of the arrow:
`Reactant A + Reactant B → Product`

Example: In `magnesium + oxygen → magnesium oxide`, magnesium and oxygen are the reactants.
