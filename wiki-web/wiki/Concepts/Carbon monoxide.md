# Carbon monoxide

Cigarettes contain tobacco. Tobacco smoke contains: - tar – a sticky, black substance that builds up in the lungs and narrows the airways - nicotine – a drug (called a stimulant) that speeds up the nervous system, making the heart beat faster and narrowing blood vessels - carbon monoxide – a poisonous gas that stops the blood from carrying as much oxygen as it should.
