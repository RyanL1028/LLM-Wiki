## Content

Oxidation is a chemical reaction in which a substance combines with oxygen. It is a key process in combustion, rusting, and respiration.

Types:
- Rapid oxidation: Combustion (burning), releasing heat and light quickly.
- Slow oxidation: Rusting of iron, food spoilage, respiration.

In combustion, oxidation of fuels produces energy. Oxidation is also a broader concept in redox chemistry where electrons are transferred.
