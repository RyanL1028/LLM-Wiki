## Content

Aid coordination involves planning, prioritizing, and implementing relief efforts to avoid duplication and ensure coverage. Led by clusters (e.g., health, shelter) under UN frameworks.

Challenges: Competing priorities, security, information sharing.

Best practices: Needs assessments, partnerships, monitoring and evaluation.
