## Content

All substances are made of tiny particles called [[Atoms]]. In the source lesson, an atom is defined as the smallest part of an element that can exist on its own.

Atoms are extremely small, with a diameter of about `0.00000001 cm`, and can only be imaged indirectly using specialized tools such as electron microscopes or scanning tunnelling microscopes.

One atom on its own does not show the visible bulk properties of a material. Properties like color, shininess, solidity, and melting happen when many atoms are present together.
