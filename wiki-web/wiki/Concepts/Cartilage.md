# Cartilage

If your bones moved against each other, they would rub, causing lots of pain. Eventually, the bone would wear away. To stop this happening, the ends of bones in a joint are covered with cartilage. This is a strong, smooth tissue. Fluid (liquid) in the joint keeps the cartilage slippery. This allows the bones to move without rubbing together.
