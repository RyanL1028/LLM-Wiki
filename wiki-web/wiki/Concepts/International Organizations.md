## Content

International organizations include UN agencies (WHO, UNICEF, UNHCR), Red Cross/Red Crescent, NGOs like MSF. They provide aid, set standards, and coordinate responses to global crises.

Roles: Policy advocacy, funding, on-ground operations, monitoring.

Challenges: Funding gaps, political interference, neutrality.
