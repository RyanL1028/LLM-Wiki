# Lipid

To test for lipids in a solid piece of food, you use a piece of filter paper. 1. Rub some of the food onto a piece of filter paper. 2. Hold the paper up to the Light. 3. If the paper has gone translucent (that is, you can see through it), the food contains lipids.
