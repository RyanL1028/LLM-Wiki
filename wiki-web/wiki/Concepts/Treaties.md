# Treaties

## Content

Treaties are legally binding agreements between sovereign states or international organizations. They are the primary source of international law, covering domains from trade and human rights to arms control and environmental protection.

Key examples include the Geneva Conventions (humanitarian law), UN Charter (founding the UN), and the Paris Agreement (climate change). Treaties are negotiated through diplomacy and enter into force after ratification by signatory states.
