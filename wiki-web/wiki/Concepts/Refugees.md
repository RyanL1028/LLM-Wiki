# Refugees

**Summary**: Individuals displaced across international borders due to fear of persecution.
**Tags**: #concepts #humanitarian #displacement #rights
**Created**: 2026-04-23T05:04:00Z
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-23T05:04:00Z

## Content

Refugees are protected under international law (1951 Refugee Convention). They cannot return home safely and need asylum.

UNHCR provides assistance, resettlement, protection.
