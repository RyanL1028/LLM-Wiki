# Bacteria

Your large intestine contains bacteria. Bacteria feed on the fibre in your diet. They make important vitamins such as vitamin K. (Vitamin K is a group of vitamins that the body needs for blood clotting, helping wounds to repair.) These vitamins are then absorbed into your body and help keep you healthy.
