## Content

Humanitarian aid encompasses food, water, shelter, medical care, and protection for vulnerable populations affected by conflict, natural disasters, or other crises. Organizations like Red Cross, UN agencies coordinate efforts.

Challenges: Access in conflict areas, neutrality, sustainability.

Types: Emergency response, development aid, protection services.
