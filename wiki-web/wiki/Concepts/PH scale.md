# PH scale

The pH scale is a measure of how acidic or alkaline a solution is. On the pH scale: - an acid has a pH of less than 7 – the lower the pH, the more acidic the solution - an alkaline solution has a pH of more than 7 – the higher the pH, the more alkaline the solution.
