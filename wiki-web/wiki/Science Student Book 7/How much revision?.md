In the third column of your table, colour in each cell – **red** for topics you find the most difficult, **amber** for topics you need to spend more time on, and **green** for topics you feel confident about. Begin with and spend most time revising your red topics, a good amount of time revising your amber topics, and less time revising your green topics.
