### What is liquid pressure?

When you swim underwater, the water exerts a pressure on you. The water molecules are pushing on each other and on surfaces, and this liquid pressure acts in all directions.

Look at Figure 2. When you squeeze (press) a bag with holes in it, the water is pushed out of all the holes because of liquid pressure. The water comes straight out of each hole and then falls because of [[Gravity]].

![A hand squeezing a water-filled bag with small holes, showing water coming out in all directions due to pressure.](images/9781382063821_graphic_431.svg)

*Figure 2The water comes out in all directions.*

> **A**
> State the angle between the flow of water leaving the bag and the surface.

If you put water in a syringe, cover the end, and try to compress the liquid, you will find it impossible. Liquids are incompressible. This is because the particles in a liquid are touching each other and there is very little space between them. Liquids pass on any pressure applied to them.

### How does pressure change with depth?

Look at Figure 4. The wall of a dam is not straight. It is wider at the bottom than at the top. The pressure at the bottom of the lake is bigger than the pressure at the top. The pressure at a particular depth in a liquid depends on the [[Weight]] of water above it. The water pressure at the bottom of the Atlantic Ocean is the same as the weight of eight cars pushing on an area the size of your thumb. Therefore, submarines need to be made of strong materials (see Figure 3).

![A heavy submarine underwater with two people sitting inside it.](images/9781382063821_graphic_356.jpg)

### Why do some things float and others sink?

Upthrust actson any objectthat is floating, or is submerged (underwater) in afluid like a liquidor gas. Figure 5 explains upthrust.

![Two objects in a tank of water with arrows showing the Forces acting on them. The object on the left floats because the upthrust force is greater than its weight. The object on the right sinks because its weight is greater than the upthrust force.](images/9781382063821_graphic_432.svg)

*Figure 5The arrows show the forces acting on an object because of its weight or the pressure of the water.*

A plastic duckfloats becausethere are lotsmore water molecules hitting the bottom of the duck than there are air molecules hitting the top of the duck. This produces the upthrust. The duck sinks (goes down) until there is enough upthrust to balance the weight.

If the area in contact with the water is too small, there is not enough upthrust to make the object float. The object will sink.

> **C**
> Explain why a very heavy ship can float.

When a submarine is underwater, there is a difference in pressure between the top and bottom of the submarine. That difference produces the upthrust.
