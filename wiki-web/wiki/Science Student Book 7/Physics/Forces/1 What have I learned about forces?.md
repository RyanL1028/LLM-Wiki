> **In this chapter, you have:**
> - learned that [[Forces]] act when objects interact and that forces come in pairs
> - explained contact forces like [[Friction]], drag forces, and tension using particles
> - explained non-contact forces like [[Gravity]] and magnetic forces using fields
> - discovered how [[Balanced and Unbalanced Forces]] explain how objects move – if forces are balanced, [[Speed]] or direction do not change; if forces are unbalanced, an object can speed up, slow down, or change direction.
