Who We Are

Student-Made, Student-Driven

Founded in 2025 at NAISHK — one student, one laptop, one idea that grew into a connected ecosystem.

🏫

NAISHK Roots

Born in a Hong Kong secondary school, SmartNexus builds tools that solve real problems faced by students and teachers every day.

🛠

Built With

Firebase, vanilla JavaScript, HTML/CSS, the WhatsApp Cloud API, the Base44 AI platform, and a lot of late nights.

🔒

Privacy First

Student data stays encrypted in Google Cloud. No trackers, no ads, no selling data — ever. GDPR & COPPA conscious.

🚀

Since 2025

What started as a single flashcard app is now a connected ecosystem of 4 products serving 50+ users across the NAISHK community.
